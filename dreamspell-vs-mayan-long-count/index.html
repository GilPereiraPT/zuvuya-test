Dreamspell vs Mayan Long Count

Explore the differences between the Dreamspell and Mayan Long Count and how both are used in Zuvuya charts.

Two Distinct Counts

The Mayan Long Count and Dreamspell Count are two distinct counts. Both are rooted in the Mayan Tzolkin but differ in origin and structure.

The Mayan Long Count

The Mayan Long Count is part of the Classical Mayan Calendric system. Its beginnings trace back to 0.0.0.0.0, corresponding to August 11, 3114 BCE in the proleptic Gregorian calendar. The historical date references in the original manuscript require final editorial verification.

“...what scholars have taken to be the Mayan obsession with time is not that at all.”— The Mayan Factor (excerpt; verify quotation before publication)

The Dreamspell Count

Dreamspell: Journey of Timeship Earth was created by José and Lloydine Argüelles and appeared as a board game in 1990. The manuscript associates the Dreamspell Count with July 26, 1987 and the Harmonic Convergence; this chronology should be checked before final publication.
