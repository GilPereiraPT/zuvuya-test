The Mayan Long Count and the Dreamspell Count

The Mayan Long Count and Dreamspell Count are two distinct counts.

Both are rooted in the Mayan Tzolkin but differ in origin and structure.

The Mayan Long Count

The Mayan Long Count, is part of the Classical Mayan Calendric system. Its beginnings trace back to 0.0.0.0.0., corresponding to August 11th 3114 BC (Gregorian Calendar) and September 6th 3114 BCE (Julian Calendar).

“…what scholars have taken to be the Mayan obsession with time is not that at all. The number sequences that intricately adorn classic Mayan monuments are primarily intended to describe calibrations of galactic harmonics as it corresponds to solar and earthly cycles of time.”The Mayan Factor
The Dreamspell Count

The Dreamspell Count began on July 26th 1987, aligning with the Heliacal rising of Sirius. It gave rise to the Harmonic Convergence, a Global phenomena in 1988. It was celebrated on the 17 th and 18th of August and became a cornerstone of the New Age Movement.

Dreamspell: Journey of Timeship Earth 2013 created by Jose and Lloydine Arguelles, appeared in the form of a board game in 1990.
