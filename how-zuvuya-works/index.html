How the Zuvuya House System Works

Learn how Zuvuya uses Kin, Tone, Solar Seal and equal houses to create a core chart with a Galactic Edge.

Creating the Core Chart Is the Doorway

Traditional astrology builds a chart based on the rotation of the Earth. Birth time and place determine what part of the sky is rising on the horizon. Zuvuya establishes its core chart from the mathematical sequence of 13/20: thirteen Tones and twenty Solar Seals.

The first layer is created by converting the birth day, month and year to a Kin number (1–260), Tone number (1–13) and Solar Seal (1–20), using either the Dreamspell Count (DSC) or Mayan Long Count (MLC).

Dreamspell examples

4/11/1974 — DSC

Kin 123 · 1+2+3 = 6 · Tone 6 · Night / Akbal

6° Virgo Rising — Night/Akbal-6 DSC

12/31/1952 — DSC

Kin 157 · 1+5+7 = 13 · Tone 1 · Earth / Caban

13° Aries Rising — Earth/Caban-1 DSC

Core chart rules

  • Tones 1–12 follow the astrological sequence Aries–Pisces.
  • Tone 13 becomes 4 / Cancer.
  • The Ascendant is never more than 13 degrees.
  • Zuvuya uses Equal Houses.
