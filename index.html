Creating the Core Chart

Traditional astrology builds a chart based on the rotation of the Earth. The birth time and place determine what part of the sky is rising on the horizon. This is what astrologers use to determine the Rising Sign and Degree of the Ascendent. Zuvuya doesn’t use the Earth’s rotation. It builds from the mathematical sequence of 13/20.

The first layer of the chart is created by converting the birth day, month and year to the corresponding Kin number (1-260) Tone number (1-13) and Solar Seal (1-20). It can convert to either the Dreamspell Count or the Mayan Long Count, both counts are derived from the Mayan Tzolkin.
