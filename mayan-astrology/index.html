Understanding Kin, Tones and Mayan Counts

Curious about Zuvuya but new to the language of Kin, Solar Seals and Galactic Tones? This short introduction will help you read the first elements of a Zuvuya chart. For detailed explanations and interpretations, you can continue to the Mayan calendar library at ZodiacRoots.

What is a Kin?

The 260-day Tzolk'in cycle combines 20 day signs, often called Solar Seals, with 13 numbers or Tones. Each combination is a Kin. In modern symbolic interpretations, the Solar Seal suggests an archetypal theme and the Tone describes how that theme is expressed.

For example, the Dreamspell chart shown in Zuvuya's Core Chart uses Kin 123 · Tone 6 · Night/Akbal. The number, Tone and Solar Seal form a signature; the remaining elements of the Zuvuya wheel explain how that signature is placed within its own house system.

Dreamspell and Mayan Long Count

Zuvuya presents two distinct chart calculations, Dreamspell Count (DSC) and Mayan Long Count (MLC). Dreamspell is a modern interpretation, while the Maya Long Count belongs to historical calendrical timekeeping; their results should not be treated as interchangeable.
