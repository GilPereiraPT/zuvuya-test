Understanding Kin, Tones and Mayan Counts

New to Kin, Solar Seals and Galactic Tones? Discover the essentials behind a Zuvuya chart, then explore the detailed Mayan calendar guides at ZodiacRoots.

What is a Kin?

The 260-day Tzolk'in cycle combines 20 day signs, often called Solar Seals, with 13 numbers or Tones. Each combination is a Kin. In modern symbolic interpretations, the Solar Seal suggests an archetypal theme and the Tone describes how that theme is expressed.

For example, the Dreamspell chart shown in Zuvuya's Core Chart uses Kin 123 · Tone 6 · Night/Akbal. The number, Tone and Solar Seal form a signature; the remaining elements of the Zuvuya wheel explain how that signature is placed within its own house system.
