How Zuvuya Came to Be

I bought my first two astrology books at the Theosophical Bookstore on Capitol Hill in Seattle Washington. Esoteric Astrology, scribed by Alice Bailey and Encyclopedia of Medical Astrology by HL Cornell MD.

Anyone can read Esoteric Astrology. There are no glyphs, no symbols, or astrological equations. It presents Astrology in such a way as to put most traditional views of astrology on their heads. It was a good place for me to start. This was not the case with the Encyclopedia. The Encyclopedia of Medical Astrology is a mixture of words, symbols and astrological correlations. I simply couldn’t read it so I went looking for an Astrology 101 class.

Astrology Et Al had a storefront in the University District. I was shown the list of classes they were offering and Jeff Green’s class caught my eye. At the time I bought my first two astrology books, I had been Gurdjieff-ing. My first husband and I were part of small Gurdjieff group and coming from that background a class on Evolutionary Astrology was much more intriguing than the 101 series.

I asked if I could sign up for the Evolutionary Astrology class. I was discouraged from doing so. It was suggested that without the basic 101 series it might be too difficult to understand or perhaps they thought it might be too frustrating for Jeff. Either way I had no intention of following that suggestion. Duly noting the day and time of class I showed up. I asked Jeff if I could sit in even with my limited understanding of astrology. He said fine. I took a seat.

Jeff was 7 years away from catching the wave to fame. His classes were lightly attended. I asked a lot of questions that usually began with “What do you mean by….”

Shortly after we became a couple.

I attended numerous classes and astrology conferences. I met some very fine and diverse astrologers. In 1985 Pluto: The Evolutionary Journey of the Soul was published. It was outstandingly well received.

Jeff became in high demand as a speaker, the book was translated into multiple languages and is a fixture in the astrological hall of fame.

In 1988 we separated and I moved to Northern California. In 1993 I was gifted a copy of the Dreamspell.

What I hadn’t been able to find through Astrology I found thru the Dreamspell.

“Astrology maps the sky around you. Zuvuya maps the Harmonic Sequence.”

I’m hoping that astrologers, dreamers and those who have an interest in their lineages, both genetic and galactic, will find the ZHS worth giving their attention too. The ZHS took paper form in 2024 and digital form in 2026. Observations, correlations and insights are at their very beginning. We welcome yours and any questions or comments you care to share.
