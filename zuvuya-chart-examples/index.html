From Core Chart to Planetary Placements

These examples are designed to show the successive layers of a Zuvuya House System chart.

  1. Core chart: Kin number, rising degree and sign, Solar Seal in the first house and Tone number at the core.
  2. House cusps: Kin numbers are placed alongside degrees and astrological signs.
  3. Solar Seals: Solar Seals are placed in the houses with their corresponding Tones.
  4. Planetary layer: Planetary signs and degrees complete the chart.
